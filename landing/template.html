Before you install

How is this better than notes after the meeting?
Argus answers while the conversation is going and you need the answer right now, not in a recap an hour later. When the conversation is over, you can still ask its record for a summary, tasks, or an email.
What does it hear and see?
The sound of the windows you pick, your microphone, and the screen or window you pick. Every line is labeled as yours or theirs. You decide what it listens to and watches.
How does it answer?
With text, code, a formula, a table, or whatever the question needs. It can also read the answer aloud, in a voice you pick. It answers questions from the call and ones you type yourself.
Which languages are supported?
The interface, speech recognition, and answers work in nine languages: Russian, Ukrainian, English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French, Turkish, and Polish. You can set the answer language explicitly, or leave it on “same as conversation” and it will be detected on its own.
Can I connect my own documents?
Yes. Add PDF, Word, and PowerPoint files, text, or photos of pages. Each session has its own materials, and Argus uses them in its answers.
So no one will notice me for sure?
Every setting in the “Invisibility” section really works, and Argus checks it itself rather than just claiming it. But it's a set of specific technical measures, not an absolute guarantee for every version of Windows and every way of recording.
Do I need an account?
Yes. You sign up with an email and password right in the app, with no email confirmation. A small welcome gift lands on your balance right away so you can try it, and there's nothing else to set up.
How much does it cost?
There's no subscription. You top up your balance from $5 and pay only for what you use: speech recognition, answers, and voice replies. Money on your balance doesn't expire. The prices are shown in the app, next to the top-up buttons. To let you try it, we give a small amount when you sign up.
Is it a finished product?
Argus is a small independent project in active development. The core works and is used every day, but some features may still be unstable.
What happens to my data?
Recordings, history, and materials stay on your computer. Audio, and questions with screen frames, are handled by third-party services; our server stores only your account, balance, and usage as numbers, with no text of your questions. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
Which programs does it work with?
Any of them. Argus hears your computer's sound and your microphone and sees the screen, so it doesn't matter where the conversation happens: a video call, a browser, or a lecture. It runs on Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) and 11; the macOS version (14+, Apple Silicon) is in beta.
My Mac says it can't verify the developer. What do I do?
The Mac version is in beta and isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first launch. You need a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later. Open the downloaded file and drag Argus into Applications. Open Argus and click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Argus. Then allow the microphone and screen & system audio recording, and restart Argus after you allow screen recording. If it still won't open, run this in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Argus.app
Windows says it protected my PC. What do I do?
The Windows version isn't signed yet, so SmartScreen warns you on the first launch. Unpack the downloaded zip first (right-click, then Extract All; don't run Argus from inside the zip), then open Argus.exe. When you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
Can I come back to a conversation later?
Yes. Every conversation stays as a session. Open its record: you get the full text, and you can ask for a summary, tasks, an email, or flashcards, then copy the answer or save it as a PDF.
