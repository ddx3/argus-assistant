Privacy Policy

Version of {{version}}. Argus is operated by {{name}} (“we”).

Argus listens to a conversation and looks at your screen, so this page says plainly where things go. Our server stores your account, your balance, and your usage as numbers. We don't store the text of your questions, your audio, or your screen frames, but they do pass through third-party services — see section 2.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

The data controller is {{name}}. For any question about your data, write to {{email}}.

2. What data we handle and where it goes

2.1. Your account (stored on our server)

  • your email address;
  • your password — only in a form that can't be reversed; we don't know the password itself;
  • the time you registered and the version of the terms you accepted;
  • your balance, top-ups, and payments;
  • usage: what you used (recognition, answers, voice replies), how much, and when — as numbers, without content;
  • the details that keep you signed in — also only in a form that can't be reversed.

2.2. The conversation: audio, screen, questions

The microphone and system audio, screen frames, recognized text, your questions, and your materials are processed on your computer. To work, part of them goes out, and third-party services handle it, not us:

  • audio goes to our server and from there to a speech-recognition service, which turns it into text;
  • a question, together with the relevant parts of the conversation, screen frames, and your materials, goes to our server and from there to an AI service provider, which prepares the answer;
  • the text of an answer goes to a voice-reply provider only if you turned voice replies on; if you use voice cloning, samples of your voice go to the same provider.

Our server does not save questions, answers, frames, or audio: it passes them on and records only how much you used, to calculate your balance. How the providers handle data after that is governed by their own rules; we choose the providers but don't control their systems.

2.3. What stays on your computer

Session recordings, answer history, frames attached to answers, and your materials are kept in folders on your computer: next to the program on Windows, and in ~/Library/Application Support/Argus on macOS (the log is in ~/Library/Logs/Argus). We don't access them. To delete them, delete those folders: on Windows, deleting the whole program folder is enough; on macOS, moving the app to the Trash does not remove them.

2.4. Payments

Top-ups go through a payment provider. We never see or store card numbers: the provider sends us the amount, the status, and a payment ID.

2.5. Technical data

Like any server, ours write the IP address, time, and requested address to technical logs. We use them for security and to fix errors. The app asks the download server whether a new version exists when it starts and then every 30 minutes while it runs. The server sees your IP address, like any request; the request carries no version number and no account details.

2.6. The website

The website has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking scripts. The description page remembers only your chosen language in your browser.

3. Why we use it

  • to provide the service and calculate your balance — this is needed to perform our contract with you;
  • to protect the service from abuse and to fix errors — our legitimate interest;
  • to keep payment records — required by law;
  • to write to you about your account: password recovery and important changes to the terms. We send no marketing emails.

We don't sell data, show ads, or build profiles from your data.

4. Who we share data with

Only those without whom the service doesn't work: an AI service provider, a speech-recognition provider, a voice-reply provider (if you turn voice replies on), a payment provider, and the hosting provider whose servers run ours. Each receives only what its task needs. We disclose data to public authorities only as the law requires.

5. How long we keep data

  • account data — as long as the account exists; after deletion it disappears from the working database right away;
  • payment and usage records — as long as accounting law requires, but without your email and without a link to you;
  • technical logs — for a limited time, then they are erased when logs rotate;
  • if we make backups of the database, deleted data disappears from them within 30 days at most.

You can:

  • get a copy of your data — write to us (address below);
  • delete your account — write to us (address below); any remaining balance is lost;
  • ask us to correct your data, restrict its processing, or object to it;
  • take your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on it;
  • complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Write to {{email}} — we will reply within 30 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data: passwords and sign-in details are stored only in a form that can't be reversed. No protection is absolute, and we can't promise one.

8. Age

Argus is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from minors; if we learn of such an account, we will delete it.

9. Other countries

The providers we use may be located in, and process data in, other countries, including ones where data protection differs from yours. We pass them only what the service needs to work.

10. Changes

We may change this policy. The version and date are at the top of the page, and the current text is always on our website at the same address. We will email you about material changes; if you keep using Argus after that, we treat the changes as accepted.
