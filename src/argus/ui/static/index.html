Before you start

Argus listens to your microphone and system audio and can look at your screen while recognition is on.

  • Recordings, history, and materials stay on this computer.
  • Speech goes out for recognition, and questions, along with screen frames and your materials, go out for an answer. Third-party services do this.
  • The people you talk to won't know an assistant is hearing the conversation. In many countries and organizations, recording a conversation needs the participants' consent, and on exams and interviews help may be banned. That's on you.
